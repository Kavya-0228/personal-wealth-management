# WealthSphere — 5-Minute Competition & Viva Demo Script
## Step-by-Step Presentation Script with Exact Timing and Action Cues

**Total Duration**: 5 Minutes (300 Seconds)  
**Presenter**: Engineering Team  
**Live Application URL**: [http://localhost:8501](http://localhost:8501)  
**Demo Profiles Used**: `DEMO-01` (Financially Strong) & `DEMO-02` (Debt Heavy) from `outputs/demo_profiles.csv`  

---

### [0:00 – 0:30] Introduction & Problem Hook (30s)
* **Visual Cue**: Have the Streamlit application open on the **◉ Overview** page showing the clean executive FinTech dashboard.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Respected evaluators, modern personal finance tools are broken. They show consumers passive account balances, but fail to answer three fundamental questions: How am I doing? What is my risk? And am I on track to reach my goals?*  
  > *Today, we present **WealthSphere**—an intelligent personal wealth intelligence platform that synthesizes supervised machine learning, transparent financial health scoring, deterministic goal modeling, and real-time counterfactual simulation into a single diagnostic engine."*

---

### [0:30 – 1:15] Executive Overview & Archetype Demonstration (45s)
* **Action Cue**: Point to the top header (`AI ENGINE ● ACTIVE`), the hero banner, and the 4 top KPI cards.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Here on the Overview screen, the dashboard immediately synthesizes the user's financial posture into four core indicators: Financial Health Score, AI Risk Profile, Goal Progress, and Monthly Surplus.*  
  > *Notice that these values are not static mockups—they are dynamically calculated in real time. For our baseline profile, the user maintains a monthly surplus of ₹35,000, representing a 43.8% savings rate, putting them in the 'Moderate' health bracket with 17.5% goal progress."*
* **Action Cue**: In the left sidebar, open **PROFILE CONTROLS**. Enter values from **DEMO-01** (Income: ₹120,000, Expense: ₹40,000, Savings: ₹300,000, Debt: ₹10,000, Goal: ₹500,000, Horizon: 5 yrs) and click **"✨ Analyze Profile"**.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"When we load our first demo profile—a financially strong saver—and trigger 'Analyze Profile', the entire pipeline recalculates instantly: Health score surges to 95.1/100 into the 'Good' bracket, and goal status confirms 'On Track'."*

---

### [1:15 – 2:00] Financial Health Scoring Deep Dive (45s)
* **Action Cue**: Navigate to **♡ Financial Health** from the sidebar radio.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Rather than relying on credit scores—which only measure repayment risk for banks—WealthSphere introduces an objective, 100-point Financial Health Score. It is weighted across six foundational pillars: Savings Rate at 25%, Debt Burden at 20%, Emergency Buffer at 20%, Expense Ratio at 15%, Monthly Surplus at 10%, and Goal Progress at 10%.*  
  > *The system dynamically explains the result: It identifies that this profile's primary strength is savings behavior and emergency runway, while highlighting debt obligations as the primary vector for optimization."*

---

### [2:00 – 2:45] AI Risk Intelligence & Explainability (45s)
* **Action Cue**: Click on **⚡ AI Risk** in the navigation.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Next is our supervised machine learning engine. We trained and evaluated Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and Decision Tree classifiers on 1,500 profiles. Logistic Regression achieved the highest performance with an **82.00% validation accuracy** and an 81.84% F1-score.*  
  > *Importantly, we do not present a black box. Below the predicted persona, WealthSphere displays the exact class probability distribution alongside our 'Why This Prediction?' explainability engine, proving that strong savings velocity and healthy emergency reserves drove the classification."*

---

### [2:45 – 3:45] The Signature Feature: What-If Wealth Simulator (60s)
* **Action Cue**: Click on **◎ What-If Simulator** in the navigation.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Now, we showcase WealthSphere’s signature capability—the **What-If Wealth Simulator**. Traditional tools force users to guess how lifestyle adjustments affect their future. WealthSphere provides a real-time counterfactual sandbox."*
* **Action Cue**: Move the **Monthly Expense** slider down by ₹15,000 and the **Monthly Income** slider up by ₹10,000.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"Notice what happens as I adjust these levers: The platform re-runs the entire data science pipeline in-memory in under 100 milliseconds.*  
  > *Our Before vs. After comparison cards immediately quantify the exact delta: Financial health score improves, goal accumulation accelerates, and the interactive Plotly multi-year projection curve dynamically shifts upward against the target threshold. This transforms passive tracking into proactive decision intelligence."*

---

### [3:45 – 4:30] Analytics, Demo Profile 2 Stress Test & Insights (45s)
* **Action Cue**: In the sidebar, quickly load **DEMO-02** (Income: ₹45,000, Expense: ₹40,000, Debt: ₹80,000, Savings: ₹5,000) and click **"✨ Analyze Profile"**. Then click **✦ AI Insights**.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"To demonstrate platform responsiveness under stress, we now load DEMO-02—a debt-heavy profile with thin cash flow.*  
  > *Instantly, the health score drops to 27.9 into the 'Poor' category, goal feasibility shifts to 'Needs Higher Monthly Saving', and the **AI Insights** engine automatically flags the debt burden in the 'Areas to Watch' section while generating an actionable, mathematically optimized Next Best Action."*

---

### [4:30 – 5:00] Conclusion, Technical Validation & Q&A (30s)
* **Action Cue**: Switch back to the **◉ Overview** screen to display the complete interface.
* **Spoken Script**:
  > *"In summary, WealthSphere has completed full automated validation: 38 out of 38 tests passed, 12 out of 12 mathematical formulas verified, and 9 out of 9 edge cases handled safely.*  
  > *By uniting predictive ML, transparent health diagnostics, goal modeling, and counterfactual simulation, WealthSphere delivers competition-ready personal financial intelligence. Thank you, and we welcome your questions!"*
